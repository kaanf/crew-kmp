# Code of Conduct

Status: original draft, not yet adopted. Before adoption, configure a confidential reporting
channel and responsible maintainers, and confirm enforcement capacity. This is a human
community policy; code quality rules belong in CONTRIBUTING.md and technical docs.

## Expected behavior

Treat contributors respectfully. Discuss implementation and evidence rather than personal
characteristics. Welcome questions and corrections. Credit others' work, respect privacy
and keep disagreement focused on the project. Do not publish someone's private data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, impersonation, deliberate
intimidation and repeated disruptive conduct are unacceptable in project spaces.
Criticism of a technical proposal is welcome; personal abuse is not.

## Scope

This policy covers repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces designated
by the maintainers. It also applies when someone explicitly represents the project.

## Reporting

Confidential reporting contact: UNVERIFIED — maintainer must configure before adoption.
Do not put private incident details in a public issue. Reports should include relevant
links, context and the reporter's preferred contact method, with unnecessary personal
information omitted. Do not invent a reporting address or direct reports to an unmonitored inbox.

## Handling reports

Designated maintainers review available evidence, limit access to those handling the case,
and avoid promising absolute confidentiality. A maintainer with a conflict should recuse
themselves; an alternate reporting route must be defined if the sole maintainer is involved.
Possible responses include requesting a change in behavior, moderating content, temporary
participation restrictions or removal from project spaces, proportionate to the incident.

## Review and appeal

Enforcement owner, alternate reviewer and appeal route: UNVERIFIED.
Maintainers should communicate the relevant rule and action to affected people where
appropriate, while protecting others' private information.
